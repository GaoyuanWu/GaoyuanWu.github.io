Hi! My name is Gaoyuan Wu, a Postdoctoral Fellow at Johns Hopkins University, working with Prof. Jan Drgona on differentiable programming and scientific machine learning for optimization and control of systems.

I recently obtained my PhD in civil engineering from Princeton University, advised by Prof. Maria Garlock. Prior to Princeton, I did my undergraduate studies in civil engineering at Tongji University with a focus in bridge engineering.

My research focuses on enhancing coastal resilience of infrastructure and differentiable physics & machine learning for optimization. Please explore my papers here.
